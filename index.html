Privacy Policy

Playtomic Manager Customer Quick Open · Last updated: 2026-09-04

Who we are

This privacy policy applies to the browser extension Playtomic Manager Customer Quick Open (the “Extension”), published for Microsoft Edge.

Publisher contact for privacy questions: jaroslaw.krajewski94@gmail.com

What the Extension does

The Extension runs only on https://manager.playtomic.io/* and adds UI helpers for club managers, such as:

  • quick links to a customer wallets page
  • quick price adjustment controls
  • wallet balance adjustment helpers

Data the Extension accesses

While you are logged into Playtomic Manager, the Extension may read information that is already available in that Manager session / page, for example:

  • player or customer display names
  • player or customer IDs
  • price or wallet balance values shown in the Manager UI
  • API responses already loaded by Playtomic Manager for tournament, schedule, or match pages

This access is used only to provide the Extension’s on-page features.

Data we collect

The Extension does not:

  • create its own user accounts
  • sell personal data
  • send Playtomic Manager data to the publisher’s servers
  • track browsing activity outside https://manager.playtomic.io/*

All processing happens locally in your browser on the Playtomic Manager website.

Permissions

The Extension requests host access only to:

  • https://manager.playtomic.io/*

It does not request access to other websites.

Data retention

The Extension does not maintain a publisher-operated database of customer or player data. Temporary in-memory caches may exist only while the page/session is open and are discarded when the page is closed or refreshed.

Third parties

Playtomic Manager is operated by Playtomic. Use of Playtomic Manager remains subject to Playtomic’s own terms and privacy policy. This Extension is an independent helper tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Playtomic unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Children’s privacy

The Extension is intended for business users of Playtomic Manager and is not directed at children.

Changes

We may update this privacy policy when the Extension’s behavior changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised when that happens.
